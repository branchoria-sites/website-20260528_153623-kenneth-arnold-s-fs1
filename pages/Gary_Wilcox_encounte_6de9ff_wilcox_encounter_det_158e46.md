## Introduction

On the morning of **24 April 1964**, dairy farmer **Gary T. Wilcox** of **Newark Valley, New York** reported a striking and highly detailed encounter with a metallic, egg‑shaped craft and two humanoid occupants. What distinguishes this story within the broader “Gary Wilcox encounter” archives is the **extended dialogue Wilcox later claimed occurred** between himself and these beings — a two‑hour interaction about mundane agricultural matters and confident but ultimately inaccurate predictions about space exploration. The following account focuses closely on Wilcox’s reported observations and the content of the spoken exchanges as they have been documented in multiple post‑event sources.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.ufoinsight.com/aliens/encounters/gary-wilcox-occupant-encounter" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinsight.com">[UFO Insight]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinsight.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Insight The Gary Wilcox Occupant Encounter – The Fertilizer Case</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO InsightThe Gary Wilcox Occupant Encounter – The Fertilizer Case - UFO InsightDecember 27, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 27, 2018</span></span></span>

## Morning Farm Work and First Sighting

At about **10 a.m. on 24 April 1964**, Wilcox was working alone on his 300‑acre farm, spreading manure with a tractor in a secluded field near the edge of woods in rural Newark Valley. While driving his tractor, he noticed an unusual shape among the trees. He steered towards it, initially thinking it might be a fuel tank or aircraft part, but as he drew closer the object resolved into an **egg‑shaped metallic structure**, roughly **20 feet long, about 4 feet high, and some 15–16 feet wide**. Its surface appeared smooth and seamless, coloured a bright aluminium‑like metal, and Wilcox said it emitted no heat, vibration or sound apart from a slight hum that reminded him of a car engine idling.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.ufoinsight.com/aliens/encounters/gary-wilcox-occupant-encounter" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinsight.com">[UFO Insight]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinsight.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Insight The Gary Wilcox Occupant Encounter – The Fertilizer Case</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO InsightThe Gary Wilcox Occupant Encounter – The Fertilizer Case - UFO InsightDecember 27, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 27, 2018</span></span></span>

Wilcox stepped down from his tractor, touched the object and even kicked it lightly, still uncertain what he was seeing. Moments later, **two small figures** — each around **four feet tall** and entirely covered head to toe in shiny white or silver suits with no visible skin — emerged from beneath it, carrying what Wilcox described as **square trays filled with soil, roots, leaves and plant debris**. They moved toward him slowly, and Wilcox later said he felt a rising sense of anxiety before they spoke.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://ufoac.com/conversation-of-ufo-passengers-with-a-farmer-1964.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoac.com">[ufoac.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoac.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">conversation of ufo passengers with a farmer 1964</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">conversation of ufo passengers with a farmer 1964</span></span></span>

## Description of the Craft and Occupants

Wilcox described the beings as humanoid in general outline but with suits that left no part of their bodies exposed. He could not discern features such as faces or hair, but he later noted that the suits had **no seams or rivets**, and that the occupants’ voices were hard to characterise. He said he **understood them**, but could not tell whether they spoke English or some other language — the voices sounded unlike anything he recognised.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://ufoac.com/conversation-of-ufo-passengers-with-a-farmer-1964.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoac.com">[ufoac.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoac.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">conversation of ufo passengers with a farmer 1964</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">conversation of ufo passengers with a farmer 1964</span></span></span>

According to accounts, it was one of these figures who first addressed him with a calm reassurance: **“Don’t be alarmed. We have spoken to people before.”** Whether that phrase was precisely as quoted or a paraphrase from later retellings, it remains the first reported verbal contact.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.ufoinsight.com/aliens/encounters/gary-wilcox-occupant-encounter" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinsight.com">[UFO Insight]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinsight.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Insight The Gary Wilcox Occupant Encounter – The Fertilizer Case</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO InsightThe Gary Wilcox Occupant Encounter – The Fertilizer Case - UFO InsightDecember 27, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 27, 2018</span></span></span>

## Reported Conversation and Predictions

Once the initial alarm subsided, the encounter reportedly evolved into an **extended dialogue** dominated by questions from the beings. Rather than a dramatic exchange about cosmic matters, the conversation focused largely on **Wilcox’s agricultural work**:

* They asked **what he was doing** and what the tractor was.
* They wanted to know **why he was spreading manure** and **what manure actually was**.
* Wilcox explained in detail the role of manure and farming practices. They also probed about **fertiliser** and organic substances that helped plant growth.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.ufoinsight.com/aliens/encounters/gary-wilcox-occupant-encounter" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinsight.com">[UFO Insight]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinsight.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Insight The Gary Wilcox Occupant Encounter – The Fertilizer Case</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO InsightThe Gary Wilcox Occupant Encounter – The Fertilizer Case - UFO InsightDecember 27, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 27, 2018</span></span></span>

Wilcox spent **nearly two hours** answering their questions, describing the mechanics of his work, the purpose of different fertilising materials, and even the general process of cultivating crops. In one later recounting, Wilcox offered to fetch a bag of fertiliser for them to examine, a proposal the beings declined on the spot because “they did not have time.” He later left a bag near the encounter site, **and when he returned the next morning it was gone**.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://ufoac.com/conversation-of-ufo-passengers-with-a-farmer-1964.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoac.com">[ufoac.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoac.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">conversation of ufo passengers with a farmer 1964</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">conversation of ufo passengers with a farmer 1964</span></span></span>

During the conversation, Wilcox also asked if he could accompany them on their craft. They allegedly declined, explaining that **their atmosphere was too thin for a human** and that they typically avoided densely populated areas because **automobile fumes interfered with their vehicles**. They portrayed themselves as visitors interested in Earth’s organic materials and conditions for growth.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://ufoac.com/conversation-of-ufo-passengers-with-a-farmer-1964.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoac.com">[ufoac.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoac.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">conversation of ufo passengers with a farmer 1964</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">conversation of ufo passengers with a farmer 1964</span></span></span>

### Predictions About Humanity’s Future

In addition to practical curiosity about farming, Wilcox later said the beings shared **predictions about human spaceflight**. According to his accounts:

* They purported that **American astronauts John Glenn and Virgil “Gus” Grissom** and two Soviet counterparts would **die within a year**, allegedly as a consequence of exposure to the elements of space.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.ufoinsight.com/aliens/encounters/gary-wilcox-occupant-encounter" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinsight.com">[UFO Insight]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinsight.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Insight The Gary Wilcox Occupant Encounter – The Fertilizer Case</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO InsightThe Gary Wilcox Occupant Encounter – The Fertilizer Case - UFO InsightDecember 27, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 27, 2018</span></span></span>

These predictions did not come true within the stated timeframe. Glenn survived many years after without fatal injury, and while Grissom later died during the Apollo 1 fire in 1967, that was more than three years after the reported encounter. Both Russian cosmonauts mentioned in variants of the story — such as Vladimir Komarov — also died later under unrelated circumstances. These discrepancies have been noted by sceptics as a significant issue in evaluating the narrative’s veracity.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.ufoinsight.com/aliens/encounters/gary-wilcox-occupant-encounter" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinsight.com">[UFO Insight]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinsight.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Insight The Gary Wilcox Occupant Encounter – The Fertilizer Case</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO InsightThe Gary Wilcox Occupant Encounter – The Fertilizer Case - UFO InsightDecember 27, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 27, 2018</span></span></span>

## Reported Departure

After concluding their lengthy line of questioning and declining Wilcox’s offer to wait for fertiliser, the two beings walked back toward their craft. Wilcox saw the object rise slowly from its hovering position, make a quiet gliding movement, then accelerate rapidly northward out of sight, leaving behind **no obvious physical trace** apart from Wilcox’s testimony and the visitors’ vanished fertiliser request.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://ufoac.com/conversation-of-ufo-passengers-with-a-farmer-1964.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoac.com">[ufoac.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoac.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">conversation of ufo passengers with a farmer 1964</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">conversation of ufo passengers with a farmer 1964</span></span></span>
